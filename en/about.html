About

Luiza-Mirabela Bordianu

Welcome!

I am an actress and symbolic civil status officer, and my mission is to turn every ceremony into an authentic, elegant, and deeply personal moment.

For over 20 years I have lived the emotion of the stage, and I believe the most beautiful stories are the ones told with sincerity. Today, I bring that experience into symbolic civil wedding ceremonies, where every word is carefully chosen, and every ceremony is created especially for the two people uniting their destinies.

I don't use standard scripts, and I don't believe in ceremonies that all look alike. I want to get to know you, learn your story, and build together a moment that truly represents you.

As a symbolic civil status officer, I officiate symbolic civil wedding ceremonies, vow-renewal ceremonies, and intercultural ceremonies, adapted to the traditions, values, and story of each couple. Ceremonies can be held in Romanian, English, French, or Italian, and are available anywhere in Romania and abroad.

Whether you'd like a ceremony by the seaside, in a garden, at a manor house, in a castle, on a yacht, or in a place that holds special meaning for you, I will be by your side to create a memorable moment.

I also organise vow-renewal ceremonies, for 10th, 15th, 20th, 25th, 30th, 40th, or even 50th wedding anniversaries — whenever you feel your love deserves to be celebrated again, surrounded by family and friends.

To me, every ceremony is more than an event. It is the beginning of a new chapter, or the celebration of a love that has stood the test of time. That is why every detail is built with sensitivity, naturalness, and care, so that you can enjoy a moment you will keep in your heart for the rest of your life.

Because every love story deserves to be told beautifully. And some stories deserve to be celebrated again.

You dream of the perfect day.
I help you turn it into reality.
